Gratitude Feels Different by the Ocean

Dear {$name},

As November settles in, Delray Beach shows its softer side. The days are warm and breezy, the ocean takes on a golden late-autumn light, and downtown fills with reasons to get together: a Thanksgiving-week 5K along the beach, international tennis at the Delray Beach Tennis Center, and Small Business Saturday on the Avenue.

Discover Casa Avenida, a boutique collection of eight luxury townhome-style residences near Atlantic Avenue, and imagine making this season in Delray part of your everyday.

🦃 What’s Happening This Month

40th Annual Delray Beach Turkey Trot 5K

November 21st

A Thanksgiving-week 5K run or walk along the oceanfront, 6:30–9 AM, starting at Anchor Park.

Billie Jean King Cup: USA vs. Hungary

November 21st – 22nd

Two days of international women’s tennis at the Delray Beach Tennis Center. Gates open at noon, first match at 1:30 PM.

Shop Small Sidewalk Sale

November 28th

Small Business Saturday downtown, 11 AM–6 PM, with sidewalk deals at independent shops and the launch of the Holiday Rewards Pass.

📅 Coming In December 🎄

100-Foot Christmas Tree Lighting & Yuletide Festival

December 1st

Old School Square, 6–9 PM. The tree is lit at 7 PM, followed by a holiday street festival on Atlantic Avenue.

Check it out!

26th Annual Downtown Delray Beach Art Festival

December 5th – 6th

A free, juried outdoor show with more than 100 artists on NE 4th Avenue, 10 AM–5 PM both days.

Check it out!

Holiday Boat Parade

December 11th

Decorated boats travel the Intracoastal from Lantana south to Delray Beach, 6–9 PM, reaching the Atlantic Avenue bridge around 8 PM.

Check it out!

Featured Local Spot

Deck 84 ↗

A waterfront classic beside the Atlantic Avenue bridge, where locals gather year-round and especially in November. Fresh seafood, a relaxed deck on the Intracoastal and dock space for those arriving by boat.
