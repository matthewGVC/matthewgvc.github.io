Version any agent
Any agent can present their own copy. Picking one makes that agent the one on the deck (cover and closing page), adds their biography page and moves to their market. An agent with an Instagram in the roster gets it in place of the team’s on the Marketing Plan and closing page. Marli’s copy also adds her Power Haus page. Picking agents by hand clears the version.

New Jersey also swaps in New Jersey stock photos where none is dropped, shows the picked agents on “Our Team”, and presents marketing as all in-house.
